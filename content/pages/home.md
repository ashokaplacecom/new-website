## About Us

The Placement Committee at Ashoka University is a student-led body working under the Career Development Office to connect students with meaningful professional opportunities. With a strong focus on quality, consistency, and integrity, the Committee manages recruiter engagement, coordinates hiring processes, and builds long-term relationships with leading organisations across consulting, finance, technology, public policy, media, development, healthcare, and startups.

Beyond facilitating placements and internships, the Committee prepares students for the professional world through CV reviews, personalised mentoring, mock interviews, resume workshops, group discussions, domain-specific training, and industry resources. Through inclusive outreach and continuous improvement, it works to align student aspirations with evolving organisational expectations and help Ashokans transition confidently into their careers.
