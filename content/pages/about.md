The PlaceCom team works closely with the Career Development Office, students, alumni, and recruiters to support Ashoka’s placement and internship ecosystem. Beyond coordinating placements and internship cycles, PlaceCom builds resources, creates opportunities, and supports students throughout their professional journey. Every department plays a distinct role in ensuring that students are well-prepared while strengthening the institute's relationships with industry.

**Internships & Skills Development**

This division supports students as they prepare for internship opportunities by helping them build the skills and confidence needed to succeed. The team mentors students through office hours, conducts resume reviews, oversees profile verification and organises preparatory workshops, equipping students for internships.

**Careers & Skills Development**

Focused on final placements, this team helps senior students navigate the recruitment process with greater clarity and confidence. The team contributes to mentorship programmes, preparation resources, verification workflows, office hours, and placement coordination, ensuring students are supported at every stage of their career journey.

**Outreach & Public Relations**

The Outreach & Public Relations team serves as the bridge between PlaceCom and the professional world. The team engages with recruiters, alumni, organisations, and industry leaders, manages external communication, and works to strengthen partnerships that create meaningful career opportunities for the student community.

**Research, Analytics & Development**

Research drives informed preparation. This division develops data-backed resources that help students better understand industries, companies, and recruitment trends. The team creates sector reports, company briefers, interview preparation material, newsletters, and placement insights that make the recruitment process more accessible and strategic.

**Media & Content Strategy**

This team shapes the voice of PlaceCom across platforms. From important announcements and recruitment updates to campaigns, event promotions, and student-focused content, the team creates clear, engaging, and impactful communication that keeps the community informed and connected.

**Events & Operations**

The Events & Operations team ensures every placement initiative runs smoothly from start to finish. The team plans and executes recruiter engagements, workshops, networking sessions, and flagship placement processes such as Day Zero and Week Zero, while managing the operational details that make these experiences successful.

**Systems & Automations**

Technology powers every stage of the placement process, and this division is responsible for building and improving the systems behind it. The team works on the PlaceCom website, internal tools, automation workflows, user experience, and digital infrastructure, creating efficient, reliable, and intuitive solutions that simplify processes for students, recruiters, and the committee alike.
